# Skilltrx — Methodology, Architecture & Final Outcomes

## 1. Data Ingestion (3 Separate Datasets, Never Mixed)
1. **Main Dataset (D1 — `med2425/resume-job-fit-merged-v1`)**:
   - **80,017 train pairs** and **13,716 provided test pairs** across 13 job domains.
   - Built from **642 unique resumes** and **280 unique job descriptions (JDs)**.
   - 3-class ordinal labels generated by **Qwen2.5-32B**: `No Fit (0)`, `Potential Fit (1)`, `Good Fit (2)`.
2. **Original Dataset (D2 — `cnamuangtoun/resume-job-description-fit`)**:
   - **6,241 train** and **1,759 test** pairs carrying original upstream labels.
   - Hash-matched against D1 (**1,004 overlapping pairs**) to measure LLM label agreement.
3. **Fairness Dataset (D3 — `sumin-yu/PopResume`)**:
   - **60,884 synthetic resumes** across 5 occupations (*Nurses, Teachers, Software Developers, Accountants, Construction Laborers*) with demographic attributes (`sex`, `race`, `edu_group`, `exp_year`, and candidate names).

---

## 2. Data Cleaning & Audits
1. **Light, Structure-Preserving Cleaning (`clean_text`)**:
   - Restores section headers glued during PDF extraction (e.g., `"SummarySelf-motivated"` → `"Summary\nSelf-motivated"`) while preserving camelCase words (`JavaScript`, `PowerPoint`, `LinkedIn`).
   - Normalizes broken bullet symbols (`•`, `●`, `▪`) into list items (`\n- `).
   - No lowercasing, stemming, or stopword removal so technical symbols (`C++`, `C#`, `.NET`) and natural casing survive for SBERT.
2. **Leakage Audit**:
   - Assigned 12-character MD5 hashes (`resume_id`, `jd_id`, `pair_id`) to normalized documents.
   - Discovered that **100% of resumes** in D1's provided test split (`test_orig`) were already present in D1's training split.
3. **Token Length Audit**:
   - Found that **99.8% of resumes** (median 1,039 tokens, p90 = 1,838) and **74.4% of JDs** (median 422 tokens) exceed MiniLM's 256-token limit.

---

## 3. Leakage-Free Data Splitting Protocol
To eliminate the 100% resume leakage in D1, the 80,017 training pairs were re-partitioned along two axes:
- **Unseen Jobs Test (`jd_test` — 8,315 pairs)**: Locked away **10% of Job Descriptions (28 JDs)** completely (`0%` JD overlap with train) to evaluate performance on brand-new vacancies.
- **Resume-Disjoint 80 / 10 / 10 Split (`StratifiedGroupKFold` grouped by `resume_id`)**:
  - **`train` (57,519 pairs)**: 513 unique resumes × 252 JDs.
  - **`val` (6,683 pairs)**: 63 unseen resumes (`0%` overlap) × 252 JDs (used only for early stopping and checkpoint selection).
  - **`test` (7,500 pairs)**: 66 unseen resumes (`0%` overlap) × 252 JDs (headline evaluation for new applicants applying to existing jobs).

---

## 4. Core PyTorch Model Methodology (`BiEncoderMatcher`)
1. **Overlapping Window Chunking**:
   - Slices documents into **256-token windows** (254 content tokens + `[CLS]`/`[SEP]`) with **stride 192** (64-token overlap), capping at **6 evenly spaced windows** per document.
2. **Shared-Weight SBERT Bi-Encoder (`all-MiniLM-L6-v2`)**:
   - Encodes each unique resume and JD in a 32-pair batch only once through the same 6-layer, 384-dimensional Transformer.
   - Mean-pools token embeddings over the attention mask per window, averages all windows of a document via PyTorch `index_add_`, and L2-normalizes into unit vectors $u, v \in \mathbb{R}^{384}$.
3. **769-D Interaction Feature Fusion & MLP Head**:
   - Forms interaction vector $z = [\cos(u,v);\; |u - v|;\; u \odot v] \in \mathbb{R}^{769}$.
   - Passes $z$ through `Linear(769 -> 256) -> GELU -> Dropout(0.1) -> Linear(256 -> 3)` to output class probabilities for `No Fit`, `Potential Fit`, and `Good Fit`.
4. **End-to-End Fine-Tuning**:
   - Trained with inverse class-frequency weighted Cross-Entropy (`[0.44, 1.97, 4.55]`), two-speed `AdamW` (`2e-5` encoder, `1e-3` head, weight decay `0.01`), 6% linear warmup, FP16 mixed precision (`torch.amp`), and gradient norm clipping at `1.0` for 3 epochs (~50.8 minutes on Tesla T4).

---

## 5. Explainability & Fairness Audit
- **Requirement Explainability (114 Skills / 23 Families)**:
  - Uses symbol-aware regex boundaries (`(?<![a-z0-9+#.])...(?![a-z0-9+#])`) so `"java"` never matches inside `"javascript"`, and `"c++"`, `"c#"`, `".net"` match cleanly.
  - Classifies each JD requirement into **Matched** (with a $\pm 70$-character word-snapped verbatim quote from the resume), **Partial Match** (sibling skill from the same family present in resume), or **Not Identified in Text**.
- **PopResume Fairness Audit (60,884 Resumes)**:
  - Scores all 60,884 resumes with the frozen model against 5 neutral occupation JDs using continuous fit score $s = P(\text{Good}) + 0.5 \cdot P(\text{Potential})$.
  - Evaluates **Observational Parity (DPD/DPR)**, **Qualification-Matched Score Gaps** within identical `(job, edu_group, exp_band)` strata, **1,500 Counterfactual Name Swaps**, and **687 JD-Swap Sensitivity Checks**.

---

## 6. Summary of Final Outcomes
- **Headline Performance**: **0.7555 Macro F1 (85.04% Accuracy)** on unseen resumes (`test`) and **0.6346 Macro F1 (81.72% Accuracy)** on unseen jobs (`jd_test`), outperforming all baselines by **+12.7 to +29.8 F1 points** (statistically significant in 1,000-resample resume-clustered paired bootstrap).
- **Near-Zero Gross Errors**: Only **0.56%** of `test` pairs (`0.67%` on `jd_test`) confuse `No Fit` with `Good Fit`.
- **Domain-Shortcut Resistance & Calibration**: Achieves **0.749 F1** on same-domain pairs vs. **0.741 F1** on cross-domain pairs, with an Expected Calibration Error (ECE) of **0.0466**.
- **Explanation Fidelity**: **100.0% verbatim quote fidelity** across **94.0%** of JDs; requirement coverage scales monotonically with predicted class (`0.177` No Fit → `0.395` Potential Fit → `0.509` Good Fit).
- **Demographic Parity & Name Sensitivity**: Qualification-matched score gaps are **≤ 0.01** (`−0.0031` Female–Male, `+0.0100` Asian/PI–White, `+0.0009` Black–White). Counterfactual name swaps have mean $\Delta \le \pm 0.01$ but flip **2.0%–5.2%** of borderline candidates, proving names should be redacted before scoring.
- **Label Validity Audit**: Qwen2.5-32B labels agree with original D2 labels on **39.64%** of shared pairs ($\kappa = 0.0687$, weighted $\kappa = 0.1718$), positioning Skilltrx strictly as human-in-the-loop decision support.
